H100 samples, then deliberately exited the maintenance controller with42.

restored all original service configuration bytes and effective profiles, and

physical GPU fault tolerance, whole-Pod HA or a successful quality cohort.

## Active matched experiment, results pending

The sole current study is `soak480-engine-quality-adapter-matched-20261026-v1`:
500 fixed public questions, excluding all18 earlier RAG cases and the100 canary
questions, repeated ABBA for2000 total direct-engine requests. Both arms receive
the same original question and top5 BM25 excerpts, without filtering for hits.
Concurrency32, KV164256, max512 natural-EOS tokens, no inference retries and an
1800s evaluation bound were frozen in advance. The independent restoration owner
is armed before the exclusive engine window. No additional resource is purchased.

The comparison measures post-retrieval engine quality, supported-exact goodput,
HTTP service and cohort-completion tails, actual tokens, GPU sampling gaps and
whole-Pod phase-wall allocation at$16.10/hour. It is not Batch/Agent end-to-end
quality, API acceptance, invoice cost or whole-platform SLO evidence. Correlated
ABBA repetitions cannot establish independent-sample significance. Packing and
ordinal encoding change together; their separate causal effects are not isolated.
Public dev has been seen in prior full engine work and is not a hidden benchmark.

differed only in floating BM25 scores and their result digest (max7.11e-15);
ordinal requests and score-stripped bodies matched. The authoritative Pod plan
was frozen before any new answer was observed, with the Mac draft and refusal
preserved. Cross-architecture numeric BM25 replay remains unqualified.
