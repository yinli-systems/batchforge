Per-device sample means were **86.377%, 96.592%, 91.491%, 85.999%**, with all
four reaching sampled 100%. These are device-wide NVML sample statistics, not
continuous Tensor Core saturation or exclusively attributed FLOPs. Sampling
covered 4,847.151 wall seconds with no gap exceeding twice the five-second
target; samples are not independent experimental replicas.

The offered rate was 0.5 task/s, 20 tasks per batch, maximum 128 in flight,
with balanced 128–16K input / 32–2K forced-output cells. Fixed output is compute
load, not a quality score. The durable-acceptance latency includes database
queueing and excludes upload/admission delay; scheduled-arrival latency includes
that delay. This offered-load study differs from the earlier burst ABBA study,
so its numbers do not establish a same-condition speedup or daily capacity.

`831b839`. Both primary enqueue paths paused and drained; only API processes
rolled; replicas and gateway passed validation; admission and watchdog resumed;
the canary stopped. Publication finished **15:20:51 UTC**. GPU worker and engine
identities remained unchanged. The [release receipt](platform480-2026-10-25/quality-api-release-20261025-v1-complete/release.json)
retains each phase independently of the performance report.

SHA-256 readback for input/output artifacts of batch indices **0, 60, 119**:
**six artifacts**. Stronger per-task attempt-history receipts also reconciled.
[Actual readback](platform480-2026-10-25/soak480-20261025-v1-complete/readback.json)
